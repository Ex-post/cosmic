The process

Five steps.
No shortcuts.

The same method used for generations, before freezers made everything faster and flatter.

01

The mix

Fresh milk and the flavour ingredients go into the inner canister. Nothing powdered, nothing pre-mixed.

Fresh milk, every morning
02

Ice + salt

The canister sits inside a wooden barrel packed with crushed ice and salt. Salt pulls the temperature far below freezing.

Where the cold comes from
03

Keep it moving

The wheel turns the canister without stopping. Constant motion keeps ice crystals tiny, so the texture stays dense and smooth.

Yes, it really works like that
04

The change

Slowly the liquid thickens, coats the walls and becomes ice cream. You can hear it change as the machine works harder.

Takes patience. Worth it.
05

Scoop

Straight from the kothi into a clay cup or a waffle cone. Made the slow way, eaten pretty quickly.

Fresh batch. Gone fast.
