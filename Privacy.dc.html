Cosmic Ice Cream ("Cosmic", "we") runs this website. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Party enquiries: your name, phone number, event type, date, location, guest count and message, only when you submit the booking form.
  • Usage data: if you accept cookies, anonymous analytics such as pages visited, device type and approximate city.
  • Cookie choice: we store whether you accepted cookies, so we don't ask again.

How we use it

  • To call or WhatsApp you about your enquiry and share pricing.
  • To understand which pages work and improve the site.

We do not sell your data. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Sharing

We only share data with the tools that run this website (hosting, form handling and analytics), and only as much as they need to work. We will share information if required by Indian law.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months after your event, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Choose "Only essential" on the cookie banner to turn off analytics.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.
